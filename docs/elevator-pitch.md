# GIHA: 30-Second Elevator Pitch

**GIHA is a two-mode web game that teaches media literacy by making you both the city manager AND the forensic investigator.**

Watch disinformation spread through a simulated population in real-time — deploy interventions to slow the spread, monitor the σ-coherence of your city's information ecosystem.

Then zoom in. Use spectrograms, frame-steppers, and source tracers to crack real-world disinformation cases. Every tool teaches a real verification skill you can use today.

Seven hundred thousand lines of browser code. No install. No login. No cost.

Just your browser, your city, and your judgment.

**Play your part at sigmatrace.io.**
