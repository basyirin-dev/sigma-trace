# GIHA: Disinformation Campaign Arc

## 6.1 The Shadow Collective

### Identity

**Name:** The Shadow Collective (self-appointed)

**Public face:** "Fighting institutional corruption" / "Exposing the truth"

**Real identity:** Financially motivated criminal network using ideological rhetoric as cover

**Structure:** Decentralized, cell-based, difficult to trace

### Motivation Matrix

| Layer | Motivation | Evidence |
|-------|------------|----------|
| **Surface** | "Exposing corrupt institutions" | Public statements, social media presence |
| **Middle** | Market manipulation, political influence | Shell companies, anonymous donations |
| **Core** | Financial profit through scams and data harvesting | Voice scam proceeds, data sales |

**Key insight:** The Collective uses ideological cover to recruit supporters who are blind to the financial exploitation.

### Methods

| Method | Purpose | Case Connection |
|--------|---------|-----------------|
| **Deepfake production** | Damage trust in institutions | Case 1 (mayor video) |
| **AI voice cloning** | Direct financial theft | Case 2 (elderly scams) |
| **Image manipulation** | Create social chaos | Case 3 (protest photo) |
| **Insider recruitment** | Access to internal data | Mira (City Hall clerk) |
| **Social amplification** | Spread content virally | All cases |

### Why They Target Veritas

| Reason | Explanation |
|--------|-------------|
| **Economic tension** | Old vs. new residents = pre-existing division to exploit |
| **Media fragmentation** | Different demographics on different platforms = easy targeting |
| **Institutional weakness** | Underfunded city government = slow response |
| **National attention** | Tech revival story = attacks get amplified nationally |
| **Symbolic value** | City named "truth" falling to lies = powerful narrative |

## 6.2 Campaign Phases

### Phase 1: "The Viral Mayor" (Case 1)

**Goal:** Undermine trust in city leadership, trigger economic anxiety

**Method:** Deepfake video of mayor saying "I resign effective immediately"

**Target district:** Foundry (rage center) → spreads to all

**Timeline:** Day 1-2 of the crisis

**Evidence package:**
- Video clip (deepfake)
- Social media posts (viral spread)
- Metadata (timestamps, source)
- Mayor's actual schedule (alibi)

**MIL lesson:** Deepfakes exploit trust in authority; multi-modal verification is essential

**Strategic impact:** R₀ spikes to 1.2, σ drops 5 points

### Phase 2: "Grandma's Distress Call" (Case 2)

**Goal:** Direct financial theft, exploit social isolation

**Method:** AI voice cloning of grandchild voices, demanding bail money

**Target district:** Harborview (elderly, isolated) → spreads fear citywide

**Timeline:** Day 3-4, overlapping with Phase 1 aftermath

**Evidence package:**
- Audio clips (voice scams)
- Phone records (call origin)
- Bank records (payment trail)
- Mira's connection (insider payments)

**MIL lesson:** AI voice cloning is accessible; verify through independent channels

**Strategic impact:** R₀ stable but victimhood rises, σ drops 3 points, reveals Mira

### Phase 3: "The Front Page" (Case 3)

**Goal:** Create city-wide social chaos, distract from financial operations

**Method:** Manipulated photo of violent protest, supposedly from Veritas

**Target district:** Uptown (status-conscious) → amplifies to all

**Timeline:** Day 5-6, climax of the campaign

**Evidence package:**
- Photo (manipulated)
- Geolocation data (wrong location)
- Lighting analysis (inconsistent)
- Original source (stock photo)

**MIL lesson:** Reverse image search and geolocation are fundamental verification skills

**Strategic impact:** R₀ spikes to 2.0, σ drops 8 points, city-wide crisis

## 6.4 Evidence Threading

### How Cases Connect

The three cases aren't isolated — they're **phases of one coordinated attack**:

| Connection | Evidence |
|------------|----------|
| **Financial trail** | Voice scam proceeds fund deepfake production |
| **Same shell company** | All three operations paid through same LLC |
| **Mira's access** | City Hall data used for all three targeting decisions |
| **Platform manipulation** | Same bot network amplifies all three pieces of content |
| **Timing** | Each case distracts from the previous one's investigation |

### Player Discovery Arc

| Case | What Player Learns |
|------|-------------------|
| Case 1 | This is coordinated, not random |
| Case 2 | There's an insider (Mira), financial motive |
| Case 3 | Everything connects, full picture emerges |

### Evidence Board Connections

Players must connect ≥3 pieces of evidence across cases to unlock the final verdict:

**Required connections:**
1. Shell company name appears in Case 1 metadata + Case 2 bank records
2. Mira's employee ID appears in Case 2 phone records + Case 3 access logs
3. Bot network signature appears in all three viral spreads

## 6.5 The Insider: Mira

### Character Profile

**Name:** Mira Petrova

**Age:** 34

**Role:** Data entry clerk, Veritas City Hall

**Access level:** Low (but sufficient for internal memos, schedules, contact lists)

**Personal situation:**
- Single mother, daughter age 7
- Daughter has rare medical condition, treatment costs $47,000
- GIHA salary: $38,000/year
- Desperate, terrified, not evil

### How She Was Recruited

| Stage | Collective Action | Mira's Response |
|-------|-------------------|-----------------|
| **Contact** | Anonymous message: "We know about your daughter" | Fear, curiosity |
| **Offer** | $50,000 + treatment covered in exchange for "small tasks" | Desperation, rationalization |
| **Escalation** | Tasks become more serious (schedules, internal memos) | Growing dread, but can't stop |
| **Point of no return** | Provides access used in all three cases | Horror at what she's enabled |

### Her Conflict

- Knows what she's doing is wrong
- Terrified of what happens if she stops
- Terrified of what happens if she continues
- Loves her daughter more than anything — this is the leverage

### Player Discovery

**Found in:** Case 2 evidence (phone records show payments from shell company)

**Key evidence:**
- Bank transfers: $5,000 from "VeraTech Solutions" (shell company)
- Timestamps align with city hall access logs
- Mira's employee ID appears in internal system

### Moral Complexity

**Player choice:** What to do with Mira?

| Option | Action | Narrative Impact |
|--------|--------|------------------|
| **Expose** | Include Mira in official report | Justice served, but Mira faces prosecution |
| **Report** | Mention insider without naming | Protects Mira, but Collective continues |
| **Ignore** | Focus only on Collective | Simplifies case, but ignores systemic issue |

**Educational value:** Teaches that disinfo ecosystems exploit vulnerable insiders — systemic solutions needed, not just punishment.

## 6.6 Win/Lose Narrative Paths

### Victory Path (σ ≥ 80, R₀ < 0.8)

**Narrative:**
> *The Shadow Collective's network is exposed. Their financial operations are traced. The insider is revealed — not as a villain, but as a victim of the same system they exploited.*
>
> *Veritas will recover. Not because disinfo was eliminated, but because its citizens learned to question, verify, and think critically.*
>
> *You didn't just solve a case. You built resilience.*

**Post-game:**
- Mira enters witness protection, daughter gets treatment
- Veritas implements city-wide MIL programs
- GIHA uses case as model for future operations

### Failure Path (σ < 20)

**Narrative:**
> *Veritas is trapped. The σ-coherence has collapsed. Citizens retreat into echo chambers, trusting only narratives that confirm their fears.*
>
> *The Collective got what they wanted: a city divided against itself.*
>
> *But this isn't the end. Every failure teaches. Next time, you'll see the patterns sooner.*

**Post-game:**
- Veritas becomes cautionary tale
- GIHA investigates what went wrong
- Player can retry with new strategies

## 6.7 Design Rationale

| Decision | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|
| Coordinated attack (not isolated cases) | Teaches: disinfo campaigns are strategic, not random |
| Financial motivation with ideological cover | Teaches: "follow the money" — question who benefits |
| Insider character (Mira) | Adds moral complexity, teaches systemic vulnerability |
| Evidence threading across cases | Rewards careful investigation, reinforces pattern recognition |
| District-specific targeting | Teaches: disinfo exploits context, not just ignorance |
| Win/lose conditions tied to σ and R₀ | Makes abstract MIL concepts tangible and consequential |

## 6.8 Implementation Notes

- Campaign phases trigger automatically based on time and city health
- Each case has intro → investigation → evidence → verdict → debrief flow
- Evidence items are discrete objects with metadata
- Connections are player-driven (drag-and-drop on evidence board)
- Verdict selection triggers strategy mode effects
- Mira discovery is optional but affects debrief narrative
